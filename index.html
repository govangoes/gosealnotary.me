Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring to my appointment?

Bring your unsigned documents, a valid government-issued photo ID, and any supporting materials requested for your appointment.

Do all signers need to be present?

Yes. Each signer whose signature requires notarization should be physically present with proper identification.

Should I sign documents before the meeting?

No. Please wait to sign until the notary instructs you during the appointment.

How do travel fees work?

Any travel or convenience fee is quoted before the appointment and must be agreed to in advance.

What are your hours?

Appointments are available daily by request. Evening and weekend times may be available depending on scheduling.
